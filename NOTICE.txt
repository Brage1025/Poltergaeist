Poltergaeist: please read before installing

Poltergaeist haunts the computer it is installed on. It builds slowly over the
first hour, then stays at its peak until it is stopped. While it runs it can:
  - type short text into the active window and erase it again
  - show ghost text and dim the screen, on every monitor
  - play breathing, footsteps and heartbeat sounds
  - shake the mouse cursor
  - set off a full-screen white flash with a loud ringing sound, at random
    times, from the moment it starts

It cannot be paused. By default it starts again every time you sign in to
Windows. To stop it:
  - End "Poltergaeist.exe" in Task Manager (stops it until the next sign-in), or
  - Restart the PC (it starts again, and the hour begins over), or
  - Uninstall it from Windows "Installed apps" (removes it completely,
    including its start-up entry and settings).
Esc dismisses any effect on screen. Ctrl+Alt+Q is an emergency exit.

Safety
  - The flash is very bright and the ring is loud. Do not install this on a
    computer used by anyone with photosensitive epilepsy, or by anyone who has
    not agreed to be haunted. Do not use headphones.
  - A ghost icon appears in the system tray whenever it is running, and it is
    always listed in Task Manager and in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.
  - Settings (volume, flash timing, turning effects off) are in
    %APPDATA%\Poltergaeist\config.json

Privacy
  - Everything runs locally. No data is sent to the internet. The optional
    AI-written whispers come from a model running on this computer (Ollama).
  - The program watches keyboard activity only to know when you pause typing.
    Typed text is never saved.
