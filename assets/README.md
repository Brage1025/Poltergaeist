## About Poltergæist-chan

The mascot, Poltergæist-chan, was generated with an AI image generator. Brage1025
chose to generate her because there was not enough time or funding to commission
a proper mascot for this project.

## Using your own mascot

Replace the two files and keep the names. Use PNGs with transparent backgrounds;
the head should be roughly square.
